Questions

Everything people ask before booking

Still unsure about something? Message us and ask — there is no charge for a straight answer.

How do I book a car in?

The fastest way is WhatsApp on +254 710 817390 — send your car make, model, year and a short description or photos of the problem. You can also use the booking form on this page and it will open WhatsApp with everything already filled in.

Do you give quotes before starting work?

Always. We inspect the vehicle (or assess the photos you send), then give you an itemised quote covering parts and labour. No work starts until you approve it, and we call you first if anything extra comes up.

Do you handle insurance accident repairs?

Yes. Accident repair and restoration is our core work. We assess the damage, prepare the documentation your insurer needs and carry out the repair to a structurally correct, properly refinished standard.

How long will my car take?

Routine servicing, brakes, alignment and diagnostics are usually same-day. Spray painting and panel work depend on the damage and paint curing time — we give you a realistic collection date before we start and update you on WhatsApp.

Will the new paint match my existing colour?

Yes. We work from your vehicle’s paint code and colour-match the mix, then blend into the adjacent panels so there is no visible edge between old and new paint.

Do you use genuine parts?

We fit genuine parts wherever you want them, and quality-branded equivalents where they make better sense for the car’s age and your budget. Either way, we tell you exactly what is being fitted and what it costs.

Can you collect my car?

If your car is not drivable after an accident, call us on +254 710 817390 and we will help arrange recovery to the centre.
